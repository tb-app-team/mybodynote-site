Privacy Policy

Hide Unwanted News (the "App")

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Summary

  • The words you register and your settings stay on your iPhone and are sent nowhere. The developer cannot see them. No account is required.
  • The Safari extension makes no network requests to the developer or to third parties. The pages you visit and the articles on them are never sent anywhere.
  • The App itself shows Google AdMob video ads, only when you choose to watch one. Google may collect data to show those ads (see "Advertising" below).

Information the developer does not collect

The developer does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • The pages you visit, your browsing history, or the content of the articles shown
  • The words you register or your settings

The developer has no server that holds your data.

What the Safari extension reads

The App's Safari extension runs only on the following sites, and only after you allow it:

  • Yahoo! (yahoo.com and its regional pages, excluding pages such as Mail, sign-in and Help)
  • Yahoo! JAPAN (www.yahoo.co.jp, m.yahoo.co.jp, news.yahoo.co.jp)

On those pages it reads the headlines and source names of the articles shown on your iPhone to compare them with the words you registered. What it reads is used only to decide whether to hide an article; it is not stored or sent anywhere.

Information stored on your iPhone

The following is stored in storage reserved for the App and its Safari extension, where other apps cannot read it:

  • The source and headline words you register
  • Settings (filter on/off, hiding comments, showing hidden articles faded, and so on)
  • The position of the ⚙︎ button
  • The number of pages you have left with every feature

The page count only counts how many pages you open. It does not record which pages they were.

If you have whole-device backups turned on, such as iCloud Backup, this information may be included in that backup. This follows a setting you chose for your iPhone; nothing is sent to the developer.

Advertising

The App's own screen uses Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google LLC, to show video ads (rewarded ads). An ad is shown only when you tap "Watch a Video". No ads are shown by the Safari extension or on Yahoo! pages.

To show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as:

  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device identifiers (such as an identifier specific to the App), device model, and OS version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information used to investigate problems

The App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) used to track you across other companies' apps and websites is not shared with Google.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some other regions, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad Privacy Settings" in the App.

Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The developer only receives aggregated figures from Google, such as how many ads were shown, that do not identify you.

When you export your settings

"Export to File" writes your words and settings to a file. You choose where it goes (iCloud Drive, the Files app, AirDrop, and so on). Once exported, the file is outside the App's control and is governed by the privacy policy of wherever you send it.

Deleting your data

Deleting the App also deletes the words, settings and page count stored on your iPhone. Export your words and settings first if you want to keep them.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
