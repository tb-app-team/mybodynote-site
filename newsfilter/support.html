Setup

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Tap Apps → Safari → Extensions
  3. Tap “Hide Unwanted News” in the list
  4. Turn on “Allow Extension”
  5. Under Permissions, tap each of the four sites (yahoo.com, m.yahoo.co.jp, news.yahoo.co.jp, yahoo.co.jp) and choose “Allow”

Open Yahoo! in Safari and a ⚙︎ button appears at the edge of the page. Tap it to open the settings.

How to use it

Adding words to hide

Under “Sources” and “Headlines” in the settings, enter one word per line and tap “Save”. Articles whose source (the outlet name shown with the article) or headline contains a word disappear from the list. Upper and lower case are treated the same, and accents are ignored, so “Macron” also matches “Macrón”.

Checking what is hidden

Turn on “Show hidden articles faded” to see the articles that would be hidden, faded with a red dashed outline. Use it to make sure nothing you wanted is being hidden. Open “Hidden on this page” in the settings to see how many articles each word hides.

Showing everything for a while

Turn off “Filter On” to show every article while keeping your words. While it is off, the ⚙︎ button shows “OFF”.

Moving or hiding the ⚙︎ button

Drag the ⚙︎ button to wherever you like. To take a screenshot without it, long-press the button or use “Hide the ⚙︎ button” in the settings. It comes back when you reload the page.

Regular expressions

Wrap a line in / to use it as a regular expression. For example, /sale|coupon/ hides headlines containing “sale” or “coupon”, and /^LIVE:/ hides headlines starting with “LIVE:”.

Price and pages

The app is free. There are no in-app purchases.

  • Pages: you start with 500 pages of every feature (unlimited words, regular expressions, hiding comments on article pages, and exporting your settings). Each Yahoo! page you open with the filter on uses one page, including going back to the previous page. You can see how many are left in the ⚙︎ settings and in the app.
  • Adding pages: open the app, tap “Watch a Video for 100 More Pages”, and watch the video ad to the end to add 100 pages. Watch as many as you like, whenever you like.
  • At 0 pages: the basic features keep working, with up to 5 sources and 5 headline words. Words after the fifth, and regular expression lines, stay saved but are turned off until you add pages.

Frequently asked questions

The ⚙︎ button does not appear

Follow “Setup” and check that the extension is on and allowed on the Yahoo! site you are viewing. If you hid the button with a long press, reload the page.

Articles are not hidden, or a whole section disappears

Changes to Yahoo!'s pages can stop the extension from working correctly. Please email us the page you were on and a screenshot, and we will fix it in an update.

The video does not load

Ad blockers and some VPN or DNS settings (such as AdGuard) can stop the video from loading. Try turning them off while you watch. A weak connection can also stop it from loading.

I watched a video but my pages did not go up

Pages are added only when you watch the video to the end. Opening the settings in Safari reads the latest count again.

Moving to a new iPhone

Use “Export to File” in the settings, then “Import from File” on your new iPhone (exporting needs pages left; importing works at 0 pages too). Your page count does not carry over; the new iPhone starts with 500 pages.

Does it work in Chrome or the Yahoo! app?

No. It is a Safari extension, so it works only in Safari. Other browsers such as Chrome, and the Yahoo! apps, cannot use it. To filter Yahoo! news, open the Yahoo! site in Safari.

Does it work on iPad?

Not yet. The app currently supports iPhone only.
