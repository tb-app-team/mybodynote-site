MyBodyNote Support

Contact

Questions, problems, and suggestions are all welcome at contact@tribest-llc.com. We read every message. Replies are written in English or Japanese.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iOS version, your iPhone model, and what you were doing when it happened. Please do not send us your records: we do not need them, and we would rather they stayed with you.

Your records

Where are my records stored?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. MyBodyNote has no account and no server. Nothing you write is sent to us or to anyone else.

Does the app use the internet?

Once at launch, to read a small file that says which version is the oldest one still supported. It sends nothing at all. The app works normally when there is no connection. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I make a backup?

Open Settings in the app and choose Export backup. The app writes a single file and hands it to the iOS share sheet, so you decide where it goes — iCloud Drive, Files, another app, wherever you like. Photos you attached are included.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

  • On the old phone, export a backup and save it somewhere you can reach later.
  • Install MyBodyNote on the new phone.
  • Open Settings, choose Restore, and pick the file.

Restoring replaces everything currently in the app. Export first if the new phone already has records you want to keep.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your records are deleted with it, because they live inside the app. There is no copy on a server for us to restore. Export a backup before deleting.

How do I delete my records?

Individual entries can be deleted from the record list. To remove everything, delete the app. Since nothing was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Using the app

Can I show my records to a doctor?

Yes. The app can lay out a period of your records as a PDF that you can print or hand over on screen. It contains what you recorded, nothing added.

Does the app tell me what is wrong with me?

No, and it never will. MyBodyNote records and displays. It does not diagnose, does not predict, does not tell you a number is high or low, and does not suggest a cause. Reading the record is for you and your clinician.

Does the app measure temperature or blood pressure?

No. You enter the numbers your own thermometer or blood pressure monitor shows, and the app keeps them together with everything else.

The Control Center buttons do not appear.

They need iOS 18 or later. On iOS 18 and above, open Control Center, tap the + at the top left, choose Add a control, and look for MyBodyNote in the list.

Can I change the language or the light and dark appearance?

The app follows your iPhone's language, in Japanese or English. For light and dark, open Settings in the app: you can follow the device setting or fix the app to light or dark on its own.

If something goes wrong

Before anything else, export a backup if you still can. Then write to contact@tribest-llc.com and tell us what happened. Please do not delete and reinstall the app to fix a problem: that removes your records for good.
